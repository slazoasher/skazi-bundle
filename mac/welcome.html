SKAZI Bundle by Asher Swissa

This installs every SKAZI plugin:

  • SKAZI: synth
  • SUBICH: bass and sub bass synth
  • BAMBA: risers and FX
  • SHAKSUKA: random chop and glitch FX
  • PITA PUMP: sidechain LFO tool
  • TAHINI: space delay and reverb
  • ZHUG: saturator and widener
  • BULLY: wavetable synth with warps, mod matrix, chaos and FX rack
  • KICKASS: kick drum synth with a browser for your Splice kicks
  • SALAT: glitch multi-FX: tempo-synced chopper, drive, crush, filter, ring mod, flanger, delay and reverb
  • TBON 303 (listed as SKAZI 303): acid bass line synth with 303 filter, accent, slide, 14 drives and a step sequencer
  • MIDI GOD: MIDI pattern generator for 10 genres and 9 roles with the ROLL BASS rolling-bass engine, plus audio to MIDI (melody, bass, drums, chords)
  • KUBE: psytrance wavetable synth with 24 KUBE-only tables, Serum .wav import, two warp slots per oscillator and 16-voice unison
  • QUAZI: 4-band fuzz with SOFT, HARD, FUZZ and TUBE per band, stereo width with mono bass, and a 3-band post EQ
  • SKACID: acid bassline synth with an evolving 16-step sequencer, MUTATE, arpeggiator and 5 distortions (Special Shigaon Edition)
  • SKILTER: morphing multimode filter (LP/BP/HP/notch) with self-oscillating resonance, LFO, envelope follower, step sequencer, drive and 150 presets
  • SKWIDE: phase-safe stereo imager that widens up to 200% and stays clean in mono, with allpass spread, phase rotation, depth, low bypass, Haas SUPER WIDE, imager, scope and 70 presets
  • SKAZI REVERB: mega reverb machine in the spirit of the classic studio racks: hall, plate, room, chamber, cathedral, spring, shimmer, reverse, gated and blackhole, echo feedback pre-delay with tempo sync, freeze, ducking, a reverse layer, a resonant tail filter and 75 presets
  • S-QUALIZER: 6-band console EQ by Asher Swissa: low cut, four dynamic bands with mid/side/left/right and sidechain, high cut up to brickwall, zero latency, natural and linear phase, band solo, EQ match, a passive tube EQ, a third-octave analyzer and goniometer, mono/side/polarity monitoring, auto gain and A/B
  • The FilterFish: filter FX

Close your DAW before installing. Afterwards, open it and rescan plugins: they are all under SKAZI.

To remove everything later, run Uninstall SKAZI Bundle in Applications > SKAZI.
